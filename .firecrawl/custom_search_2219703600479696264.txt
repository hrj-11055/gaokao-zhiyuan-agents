The university covers an area of over 1, 310 mu (approximately 873 ,333 square meters) with a building area of 570,000 square meters. It currently has 18,758 full-time students and 1,2 53 faculty and staff members, including 5 30 personnel with senior professional titles or higher and 8 14 teachers holding doctoral or master's degrees. Four experts enjoy special allowances from the State Council. The university comprises 13 secondary colleges offering 42 undergraduate majors across 9 disciplines, including literature, science, education, engineering, agriculture, and management. The n otable academic resources include : 1 national first-class undergraduate program , 2 national first-class undergraduate courses , 1 national science practice education base , 5 provincial key disciplines , 19 provincial first-class undergraduate programs , 41 provincial first-class undergraduate courses , 9 provincial characteristic programs , 10 provincial teaching teams , 27 provincial excellent courses , 2 provincial key laboratories , and 2 provincial key laboratories in colleges and universities . It is a founding member of the “ Belt and Road ” University Strategic Alliance. The university has dispatched students to study in the USA, Belarus, Ukraine, Romania, and other countries. Currently, it enroll s international students from Russia, Laos, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and other countries. The university offers the Longdong University President’s Scholarship for International Students to encourage outstanding students from around the world to pursue their studies here.
