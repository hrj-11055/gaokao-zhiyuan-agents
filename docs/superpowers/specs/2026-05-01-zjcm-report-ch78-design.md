# Design Document: ZJCM Deep Research Report (Chapters 7 & 8)

## 1. Project Overview
This project involves the deep writing of the final two chapters of the "Zhejiang Conservatory of Music (ZJCM) Deep Research Report". The goal is to provide a sharp, objective, and pragmatic analysis for prospective students and parents, exceeding 2000 words.

## 2. Content Structure

### Chapter 7: 风险提示——华丽背后的“窄门”与“围城”
*   **7.1 竞争风险：双高时代的“末位淘汰”**
    *   Focus on the rising cultural score requirements (490+ for school exams).
    *   The "Hangzhou Magnet": How the city's popularity inflates competition beyond local capacity.
*   **7.2 灵活就业风险：在“网红之都”的幻象中寻找出口**
    *   Deconstructing the "Instant Stardom" myth.
    *   Analysis of the high cost of living in Hangzhou vs. freelance income instability.
*   **7.3 数字化管理的“束缚感”：当艺术遇上精密治理**
    *   Critical review of the Smart Campus system.
    *   The tension between administrative efficiency and artistic spontaneity.
*   **7.4 资历断层风险：十年新校的“人脉荒原”**
    *   The lack of alumni power in traditional industry nodes.
    *   Comparison of "Guanxi" networks with CCOM and SHCM.

### Chapter 8: 总结评价——新锐的野心与考生的终极抉择
*   **8.1 六维量化雷达图：数据驱动的价值回归**
    *   Detailed justification for scores in Hardware, Location, Faculty, Employment, Heritage, and Synergy.
*   **8.2 横向三校博弈：上音、武音与浙音的错位生存**
    *   Heritage vs. Innovation.
    *   Academic stability vs. Market agility.
*   **8.3 最终报考建议：给不同家庭的“清醒剂”**
    *   Advice segmented by student goals (Classical vs. Pop vs. Tech).
    *   Risk-adjusted strategies for different score tiers.

## 3. Style & Tone
*   **Sharp (犀利)**: Direct address of uncomfortable truths about the art industry.
*   **Objective (客观)**: Balancing the top-tier hardware with the lack of historical depth.
*   **Pragmatic (务实)**: Focusing on ROI, employment reality, and score tiers.

## 4. Technical Specifications
*   **Format**: Markdown.
*   **Target File**: `tmp_ch78.md`.
*   **Word Count**: >2000 words (total for both chapters).
